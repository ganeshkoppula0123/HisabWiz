YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE

Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

HisabWiz calculators work without an account. Optional Supabase sign-in and profiles are available only when the app owner configures a Supabase project. You can continue using all calculators without registering.

Information and calculations

Calculator inputs and results, including age, date, unit, bill-splitting, and savings calculations, are processed on your device. Exact birth dates are not included in calculation history. The daily encouragement wheel also runs locally and does not send or save a result. HisabWiz does not send inputs or results to a HisabWiz server. The app does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Recent calculations

If you use the recent-calculations feature, up to ten summaries are saved in this browser’s local storage on your device. Use “Clear history” in the app to remove them, or clear this site’s browser data. Browser storage is not encrypted and may be visible to anyone with access to your device or browser profile.

Optional accounts

If account sign-in is enabled and you choose to register, your email address and display name are sent to the configured Supabase project for account and profile management. Authentication sessions may be stored in this browser by the Supabase client so you can stay signed in. Your password is sent to Supabase for authentication and is not saved by HisabWiz. The Supabase project owner and Supabase process account data under their applicable terms and privacy practices. You can use the app without an account; calculations and calculation history are not sent to Supabase.

Copy and offline use

When you choose a copy button, the app requests permission from your browser to copy the displayed summary to your clipboard. If installed or served over a supported secure connection, the app may cache its static files on your device so the calculators can open offline. Account sign-in requires an internet connection. No calculation history is included in that app cache.

Third-party services

HisabWiz does not include third-party fonts, analytics, ads, or embedded tracking services. When Supabase credentials are configured, the Supabase JavaScript client is loaded from a CDN and account requests go to the configured Supabase project. Without that configuration, no Supabase client is loaded. Your browser, device platform, CDN, Supabase, or hosting provider may handle technical requests under their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact provided with the app’s official store listing.
